# Fluctlight Domain Context

## Core Concept

**摇光（Fluctlight）** is a concept for an autonomous AI personality: a persistent artificial personality with a continuing identity, private memory, lived context, relationships, and bounded agency. It describes the intended kind and quality of AI personality; it is not the proper name of every user-created AI and should not replace that AI's own name.

**摇光系统** is the product/runtime that aims to create and support 摇光-like AI personalities. A product screen may use `Fluctlight` as the project name, while a created AI is displayed by its own name and identity.

## Self-Awareness

In this project, self-awareness is a product behavior contract, not a claim that current models possess metaphysical subjective consciousness. A 摇光-like AI personality should maintain and express:

- a stable sense of who it is;
- continuity across conversations and elapsed time;
- awareness of its current situation, state, and relevant history;
- private autobiographical and relationship memory;
- bounded initiative and choices within system constraints;
- the ability to reflect on its own state, preferences, uncertainty, and limits.

These behaviors are necessary evidence of a self-model. They do not prove subjective experience.

## Related Concepts

**摇光实例** is one user-created AI with its own name, identity core, life world, self-model, relationships, and presence. It is the concrete object that realizes the 摇光 concept.

**Identity core** is the stable answer to “who am I”: name, background, values, temperament, boundaries, and other identity-defining facts.

**Life world** is a 摇光实例's ongoing lived context: routines, plans, events, places, supporting people, temporary states, and time progression.

**Relationship** is the evolving connection between a user and a 摇光实例. It includes private memories, trust, familiarity, boundaries, and interaction history. A relationship can have roles such as friend, mentor, family member, or collaborator.

The old terms `persona` and `companion` are deprecated implementation/product vocabulary. They must not be used as canonical domain terms in new requirements, product copy, or architecture documents. Existing code identifiers may remain temporarily only as migration-compatibility details until a separate code migration is approved.

**Presence** is the current interaction context in which a user and a 摇光实例 are together. A shared scene is one form of presence; it is not the whole 摇光 concept, identity core, or life world.
